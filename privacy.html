NPC Advisory No. 2020-1 Compliance

Privacy Notice & Exam Policies

Effective Academic Year 2026–2027 • Information Technology Department

1. Purpose Limitation

This exam portal collects and processes your student credentials, submitted responses, and technical telemetry exclusively for the administration, evaluation, and record-keeping of your academic examinations. Your data will never be utilized for commercial purposes or disclosed to third parties outside the educational institution.

2. Data Collected & Proctoring Telemetry Disclosure

When you sit an examination on this platform, the following data points are processed:

  • Identification: Verified Google institutional email, student ID number, full name, year level, course, and section.
  • Academic Answers: Responses to questions, choice selections, and submission timestamps.
  • Operational Telemetry: Connection heartbeats, tab focus-loss instances (page blur), and duration away from the active exam window.

Important Note on Telemetry: In compliance with fair assessment principles, focus-loss counts and question timings constitute operational telemetry to assist instructors in diagnosing technical difficulties. Telemetry is non-adjudicative and does not serve as automated proof of academic dishonesty without faculty inquiry.

3. Data Retention Schedule

Examination submissions, question breakdowns, and score histories are stored securely in departmental academic records for a duration of one (1) academic year following completion of the term. Upon expiration of the retention window, raw answer logs are securely purged.

4. Student Grade Dispute & Technical Incident Process

If you encounter a network failure, premature session timeout, or wish to dispute an automated grade:

  • Notify your instructor immediately with your Student ID and Exam Code.
  • In the event of an unresolvable technical discrepancy, students may request an audit of their attempt timestamps and recovery journal through the Department Head.
